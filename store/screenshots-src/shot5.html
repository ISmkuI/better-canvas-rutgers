In pandas, what is the difference between .loc[] and .iloc[]?
.loc[] selects rows and columns by label (index names, column names or a boolean mask); .iloc[] selects by integer position. Both accept slices, but a .loc slice includes the end label while an .iloc slice excludes the end position.
