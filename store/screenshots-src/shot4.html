Homework 3: Energy and Work

Due Sep 15 by 11:59pm Points 20 Submitting a file upload Available Sep 8 at 12am - Sep 15 at 11:59pm
Start Assignment

Show all work. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and give final answers with units and two significant figures.

A 2.0 kg block is released from rest at the top of a frictionless ramp that is 3.0 m above the floor. At the bottom of the ramp the block slides across a horizontal surface and runs into a spring with spring constant k = 800 N/m.

  1. Find the speed of the block at the bottom of the ramp.
  2. Find the maximum compression of the spring.
  3. Now suppose the horizontal surface has a coefficient of kinetic friction μk = 0.20 and the spring sits 1.5 m from the base of the ramp. How does your answer to part (b) change?

✨ Study Assistant claude-sonnet-4-5
A 2.0 kg block is released from rest at the top of a frictionless ramp that is 3.0 m above the floor. At the bottom of the ramp the block slides across a horizontal surface and runs into a spring with spring constant k = 800 N/m.
What this tests: conservation of mechanical energy (gravitational PE → kinetic energy → spring PE), and where friction breaks that conservation.

• Part (a): the ramp is frictionless, so mgh = ½mv². Notice that the mass cancels — what does that tell you about the answer?
• Part (b): at maximum compression the block is momentarily at rest, so all of the kinetic energy from (a) has become ½kx². Set the two equal and solve for x.
• Part (c): friction removes energy W = μk·m·g·d over the 1.5 m before the spring. Subtract that from the kinetic energy first, then repeat the step from (b).
